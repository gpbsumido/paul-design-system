# Chart palette

Two palettes, doing two different jobs. Picking the wrong one is the most common
way a correct chart still misleads.

- **Categorical** — `--paul-chart-1..6`. Identity: which series is which.
- **Sequential** — `--paul-chart-seq-1..5`. Magnitude: how much, on ordered data.

Assigned in fixed order, never cycled. Slot 1 is always the first series.

**Slot order is part of the contract.** Adjacent slots are the pairs a reader has
to tell apart, so the order is what gets validated, not just the set. Reshuffling
these without re-running `packages/tokens/src/__tests__/chart-palette.test.ts`
can quietly reintroduce a pair nobody can distinguish.

That is not hypothetical. The palette this replaced ran blue at slot 1 and purple
at slot 2 — the first two series of every multi-series chart — at **ΔE 1.3** under
deuteranopia and **12.0** for normal vision, against a hard floor of 15. It also
ended on a step that sat outside the lightness band and below the chroma floor,
so slot 6 read as gray.

| Check | Light | Dark |
|-------|-------|------|
| Lightness band (OKLCH L) | all six inside 0.43–0.77 | all six inside 0.48–0.67 |
| Chroma floor | all ≥ 0.10 | all ≥ 0.10 |
| Worst adjacent pair, simulated CVD | ΔE 19.0 | ΔE 16.8 |
| Worst adjacent pair, normal vision | ΔE 27.2 | ΔE 27.2 |
| Contrast vs chart surface | all ≥ 3:1 | all ≥ 3:1 |

Dark is a **separately chosen** set of steps, not a flip of the light ones — the
dark lightness band is tighter, so the light values sit too dark against a dark
surface.

## Sequential ramp

One hue, light to dark. For anything with a natural order: cohort heatmap cells,
funnel stages, tiers, age bands.

Putting the categorical palette on ordered data double-encodes the value as hue —
it spends the one free channel on information the chart already shows through
length or position, and it reads as noise.

## Rules

- **Past six series, don't invent a seventh colour.** Fold the tail into "Other",
  facet into small multiples, or split the chart. A generated hue is
  indistinguishable from one of these six under colour vision deficiency.
- **Colour follows the entity, not its rank.** A filter that removes a series must
  not repaint the survivors — a reader who learned "Acme is blue" is now misled.
- **Status colours are not series colours.** Green, amber and red carry meaning
  (good / warning / critical) and ship with a text label, never colour alone. The
  categorical slots draw from the same ramps at different steps, for separation,
  not for meaning — a green *series* does not mean "good".
- **Text wears text tokens.** Values, labels and legends stay in foreground or
  muted ink; the coloured swatch beside them carries the identity.

## Overriding

Every slot is a custom property on `.paul-chart`, so a consumer can re-skin the
whole set from an ancestor:

```css
.brand-charts {
  --paul-chart-1: #123456;
  --paul-chart-2: #654321;
}
```

If you do that, run the same checks. The validator lives in the dataviz skill and
its maths is ported into `packages/tokens/src/__tests__/palette-check.ts`.
