# Color Palette

Verdigris and ember on warm paper. The identity ramps are the two brand hues;
the neutral ramp is a warm gray rather than a true one, which is why the
semantic surfaces below are paper and ink rather than #ffffff and #000000.

## Label Tokens

The ink a component paints on a fill it painted itself, rather than on the page.
These exist because those labels used to be unreachable: `.btn--primary` wrote
`color: #fff` straight into the stylesheet, which assumes the primary is dark
enough to carry white. On a light brand colour that measures around 2:1, and the
only fix available was to override the component's own selectors.

| Token | Light | Dark | Painted on |
|-------|-------|------|------------|
| `--paul-color-on-primary` | `#ffffff` | `#ffffff` | primary-600, primary-700 on hover |
| `--paul-color-on-primary-tint` | primary-700 | primary-200 | primary-50/100 light, primary-900/950 dark |
| `--paul-color-on-error` | `#ffffff` | `#ffffff` | error-600, error-700 on hover |
| `--paul-color-on-error-tint` | error-700 | error-200 | error-100 light, error-900 dark |
| `--paul-color-on-success-tint` | success-700 | success-200 | success-100 light, success-900 dark |
| `--paul-color-on-warning-tint` | warning-700 | warning-200 | warning-100 light, warning-900 dark |
| `--paul-color-on-inverse` | `#ffffff` | neutral-900 | neutral-900 light, neutral-100 dark |

Every default reproduces exactly what the components painted before these tokens
existed, so adding them changes nothing until one is set.

`on-primary-tint` is the sharper half of the problem. The pale-fill family — the
secondary button, the info badge, the avatar fallback — took its label from
`primary-700`, which is also the primary button's hover fill. One step cannot
both stay a saturated brand fill and be dark enough to read on a 50 tint; on a
light brand colour those requirements pull apart and nothing satisfies both.
`error-700` had the identical collision between `.badge--error` and
`.btn--danger:hover`.

Re-pointing a ramp means owning the pair. The full list of what has to stay
readable, with the ratios this palette ships, is in `contrast-notes.css`, and
both halves are enforced: `tinted-contrast.test.ts` measures the ratios,
`label-tokens.test.ts` measures that the labels are reachable at all.

```css
/* A light brand colour needs a dark label, not white. */
:root {
  --paul-color-primary-600: #f2c14e;
  --paul-color-on-primary: #2a1f00;
}
```

## Usage

```css
.my-element {
  color: var(--paul-color-primary-600);
  background: var(--paul-color-surface);
  border: 1px solid var(--paul-color-border);
}
```
